# QUANTUM music generator
## 100% vibe coded
## But I generated the ideas

Raw DOOM MIDI music used for training was obtained from [here](https://www.os2site.com/sw/games/action/doom/index.html). Thanks the hosts.      
Three notebooks, three approaches: independent sampling of notes distribution, Markov chain-like sampling and Markov chain-like with rotation gates as "synthesizer" controls.       
The notebooks also generate music under simulated quantum noise. I was wondering how that affects the outcome.       
Along the way the notebooks also generate comparison plots and divergence values to understand how the quantum notes distribution differs from the input one.  
The same goes for entanglement values - they let us analyze how entangled the note components are.
